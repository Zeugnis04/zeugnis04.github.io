Many seem to agree on the *melancholic* and *weary* sounds of the album, and I would say I do kinda agree, but just not very sure why I feel so. The steps they make in this album and the layers of sounds consisting this album are indeed intriguing.

My favorite track of the song is definitely Endsong. Endsong starts with a quite lengthy{%sidenote '' 'not overwhelming though'%} instrumental build up of sounds, which does not proceed significantly for 143 seconds to the next guitar riff section. You can definitely feel something is coming to an end. Obviously, the album. And perhaps, the band itself. Even for ones who did not have the chance to grow up listening to The Cure{% sidenote '' 'unfortunately including myself'%}, one can still resonate with the well-written *cosmic* farewell.

Something that is quite underwhelming would be that some of the sounds simply do not suit the songs very well. I feel some of the instruments utilized here are not produced properly, particularly as mentioned by some, the drums could be annoying.

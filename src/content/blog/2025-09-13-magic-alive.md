Is this jazz rap? I am not sure. Though referred to as jazz rap by many, I just don't really feel like *jazz* of *jazz* rap being *jazz* jazz. Anyways, I do embrace the maximalist, lush, vibrant, and energetic kind of music showcased in this album. If people call that *jazz*, let it be so.

As in the excerpt, this album is *emotional and powerful yet unique*. The momentum created from the opening tracks of the album is carried throughout the album, despite the songs varying in density and tempo. Overall the progression feels organic and persuasive. Magic, Alive! feels like one of the most magical pieces I have listened to recently. The track, complex and full of layers flows eloquently and proceeds very powerfully. The momentum that McKinley Dixon carries in this specific track is massive and suits well the lush and rich orchestration of the track.

I am definitely willing to see much more coming from him.

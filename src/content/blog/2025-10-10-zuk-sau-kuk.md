From 王家衛 (Wong Kar-Wai)’s movie 墮落天使 (*Fallen Angels*).

This Cantonese song is sometimes known as the *traditional* birthday song in Cantonese-speaking regions, but I was not sure about that claim. Meanwhile some sources including [Cantonese Wikipedia](https://zh-yue.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%A5%9D%E5%A3%BD%E6%AD%8C) and [Baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%A5%9D%E5%AF%BF%E6%9B%B2/3556788) claimed that this song is from the album 歡樂年年 (1977), while this turned out to be wrong. Descriptions from [this YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JUG0b10WikY) suggest that this song is from 商台歌集第一輯 (1966) by 香港商業電台{% sidenote 'explanation of radio company' 'Commercial Radio Hong Kong, one of the two commercial radio broadcasting companies in Hong Kong, along side Metro Radio Hong Kong. Founded 1959.'%}. Full explanation from the video is written below.

In short, it seems to be a song produced by a Hong Kong radio company to celebrate its 10th anniversary. Interestingly, the album it comes from was among the first Cantonese song albums ever released.

<p lang="zh-Hant">60年代初，香港社會漸漸現代化，工商業都發展蓬勃，尤其是工廠，帶來很多就業機會，一些女工在工廠生產之餘，陪伴她們一起工作的便是收音機。收音機每天傳來電台的廣播劇，除了故事吸引，主題曲也動聽。不過當時社會上流行的是比較典雅的海派華語時代曲或新潮的歐西歌曲。粵語歌曲不算有市場，而且往往令人感到它們比較低俗，是沒多受教育的低下層勞動者才會喜歡。1952年第一批由和聲唱片出版的粵語歌曲，便不是為香港，而是為了新加坡市場而設的。每當這些廣播劇新曲播出之際，電台都會收到數千封來信索取歌詞。但沒有唱片公司夠膽量為這些歌曲發行唱片。所以1969年由香港商業廣播有限公司（現簡稱商台）為了慶祝啟播十周年，遂出版這張《商台歌集第一輯》，把一些深受歡迎的歌曲灌錄成唱片，但有趣的是自此卻沒有發行第二輯了。</p>

<p lang="zh-Hant">60年代香港的播音事業，是商業電台和香港電台的競爭。前者較注意年輕人的音樂潮流。在60年代，商台的播音歌手產生得比較多。尹芳玲相信是60年代播音歌手的表表者。她除了是出色的播音員，也唱過不少商台廣播劇主題曲，如〈薔薇之戀〉、〈曲終殘夜〉、〈情如夢〉。另外，她也唱過許多電影歌曲，更曾主演過電影《廣播皇后》、《春曉人歸時》、《紅燈綠燈》等。同年代的播音歌手，還有莫佩文、梁美嫻、翠碧，都是商台的。</p>

<p>In the early 1960s, Hong Kong society was gradually modernizing, and industry and commerce were booming — especially factories, which created many jobs. Alongside their production work, some female factory workers were accompanied by the radio. Every day the radio broadcast serialized dramas: beyond the engaging plots, the theme songs were catchy. But at the time, what was fashionable in society were the more elegant Shanghai-style Mandarin historical drama (時代劇) numbers and the new Western pop. Cantonese songs weren't seen as a real market; they were often considered rather vulgar — music liked by lower-class laborers with little education. In 1952 the first batch of Cantonese songs released by 和聲唱片 wasn’t even intended for Hong Kong, but for the Singapore market. Whenever a new theme song from those radio dramas aired, stations would receive thousands of letters asking for the lyrics. Yet no record company dared to release them on disc. So in 1969, to mark its tenth anniversary, 香港商業電台（商台）put out 《商台歌集（第一輯）》, recording some of the most popular numbers — though interestingly, no second volume ever appeared.</p>

<p>In the 1960s, broadcasting in Hong Kong was a competition between 商台 and 香港電台; the former paid closer attention to youth music trends. During that decade, many "broadcasting singers" emerged from 商台. 尹芳玲 was arguably the standout of the era. Besides being an excellent announcer, she sang numerous 商台 radio-drama themes—such as 《薔薇之戀》, 《曲終殘夜》, and 《情如夢》. She also recorded many film songs and even starred in movies including 《廣播皇后》, 《春曉人歸時》, and 《紅燈綠燈》. Other singers of the same period included 莫佩文, 梁美嫻, and 翠碧, all associated with 商台.</p>

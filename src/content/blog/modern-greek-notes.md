The phonological status of the Greek voiced stops [<span lang=ipa> b d g </span>] is disputed --- voiced stops (at least in the past) used to be produced with a preceding homorganic nasal e.g., [ <span lang=ipa> mb nd ŋg</span> ], or by some, prenasalized e.g., [ <span lang="ipa">ᵐb ⁿd ᵑg</span> ], but recent studies suggest that nasality rarely accomapnies voiced stops any more.
